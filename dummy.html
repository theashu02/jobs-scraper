[Remote] Software Engineer 5 - Member, Commerce & Games Engineering

Posted Sep 20, 2026

Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. Netflix is a company on a mission to entertain the world, and they are seeking Software Engineers to enhance their backend systems. The role involves solving complex distributed systems problems and driving large projects from inception to production.

Responsibilities

  • Work on many of our core backend systems that directly impact our API platform, messaging, personalization, sign-up, partnership, payments, and billing systems
  • Solve challenging problems with direct business impact across a wide variety of complex distributed systems
  • Work cross-functionally and drive large projects throughout their lifecycle from identifying the problem to launching in production

Skills

  • 6-8+ years of professional experience designing, building, and shipping software
  • Skilled in object-oriented programming and write excellent code
  • Own what you build, beyond just your code. Have a passion for quality
  • Proficient in systems design. Can craft systems and solutions to realize a range of product and engineering goals
  • Experience working with large-scale, low-latency distributed systems
  • Love driving real business impact by applying software engineering, analytical, and collaborative skills across engineering, product, design, ML/data science, and consumer insights
  • Can work through ambiguity, multi-task effectively, and are highly motivated to lead cross-functional projects and deliver high-quality solutions
  • Strive to improve yourself and help your colleagues grow. Co-workers think of you as someone who is kind and does excellent work
  • Communicate effectively in both written and verbal form. Be a great listener and give all voices an opportunity to express themselves
  • Self-driven and impact-oriented. Proactive and can move fast or pivot to meet the changing needs of the product

  • Full-time salaried employees are immediately entitled to flexible time off
